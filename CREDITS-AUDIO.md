# Audio credits

Every sound and music track in Moleforge is a recording from a free, properly licensed pack on
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org) (nothing is synthesized). The files were trimmed, mixed to
mono, loudness-normalised, some re-pitched or cut into seamless loops, and encoded as MP3.

**CC0** (public domain) packs need no attribution; they are listed anyway with thanks.
**CC-BY 3.0** sounds (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) require credit, given here:

- "Chicken Sound Effect" by **IMadeIt**, CC-BY 3.0, https://opengameart.org/content/chicken-sound-effect (edited: trimmed, mono, normalised, cut into short pieces)
- "Rodent or Alien Speaking (Cute, Weird) (Yo Frankie!)" by **Lamoot**, CC-BY 3.0, https://opengameart.org/content/rodent-or-alien-speaking-cute-weird-yo-frankie (edited: trimmed, mono, normalised, cut into short pieces)
- "Yawn" by **spookymodem**, CC-BY 3.0, https://opengameart.org/content/yawn (edited: trimmed, mono, normalised, cut into short pieces)
